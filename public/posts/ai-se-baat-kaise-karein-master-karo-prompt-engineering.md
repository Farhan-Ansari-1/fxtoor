Hey dosto! Sab log **AI** tools jaise **ChatGPT**, **Bard** use kar rahe hain na? Par kya aapko pata hai ki unse *best answers* nikalne ka ek secret hai? Wo hai **Prompt Engineering**!

Socho, ye **AI** models, yaani **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, ekdum brilliant students ki tarah hain. Unse sahi answer nikalwane ke liye, *sahi sawaal पूछना* bahut zaroori hai. Yahi **Prompt Engineering** hai – *the art and science* of crafting effective prompts to get the desired output from **AI**. Yeh sirf 'hello' bolne se kahin zyada hai. Isme aapko clear instructions dene hote hain, context provide karna hota hai, aur sometimes example bhi dena padta hai. Tab jaakar **AI** aapko woh cheez degi jo aap *actually* chahte ho.

College life mein kitna kaam aata hai!
*   Assignment mein stuck ho? Ek accha prompt likho, research points mil jayenge.
*   Project ka code optimize karna hai? Sahi prompt se suggestions le sakte ho.
*   Resume banwana hai ya internship ke liye cover letter? **LLM** se draft karwa sakte ho, bas *achhe prompts* do.
Yeh skill future ke liye bhi super important hai, kyuki **AI** har field mein infiltrate kar raha hai.
